# 🏢 B2B Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze B2B sales dynamics, pipeline performance, sales rep productivity, and product-level revenue distribution.

![B2B Sales Dashboard]()

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This B2B Sales Dashboard offers comprehensive analytics into sales deal pipelines, revenue contributions by managers and sales agents, deal size distributions, and product performance. It enables account managers and sales leaders to track performance KPIs and make data-driven decisions.

## 📈 Visualizations Included

* **Executive KPI Cards:** Dynamic display of Avg Median, Active Deals, Avg Deal Value, and Total Deals.
* **Top Manager By Total Sales:** Horizontal bar chart comparing revenue generated across management teams.
* **Total Sales By Sales Agent:** Horizontal bar chart ranking sales representatives by closed sales value (`Sum of close_value`).
* **Distribution By Size:** Donut chart categorizing deals across size categories (*No Revenue, Large, Medium, Small*).
* **Sales Revenue By Product/Sector:** Column chart evaluating total closed revenue segmented by deal stage (*Won vs. Lost*) across product lines (*GTX Plus Pro, MG Advanced, GTX Plus Basic, GTX Basic, MG Special*).
* **Account Slicer:** Top-right interactive filter to slice performance data by specific corporate accounts (e.g., *Acme Corporation*).
